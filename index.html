Sthala privacy policy

Effective 11 November 2026

Sthala is an offline app about India's temples, made for children and the grown-ups who sit with them. It collects nothing about you or your child.

  • No account. There is nothing to sign up for or log in to.
  • No data leaves your phone. The app does not use the internet at all (it has no internet permission). What your child has heard and found (stories, medallions, settings) is saved only on your device, and is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no third-party code that collects data.
  • The reading voice. Stories are read aloud by your phone's own text-to-speech voice, on the device. Nothing is recorded; the app never uses the microphone or camera.
  • No links out. Nothing in the app opens a website or another app.
  • Children. The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.
  • Changes. If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
